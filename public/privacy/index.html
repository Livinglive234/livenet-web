Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

This policy covers the LiveNet website at livenet.host and livenet.beer (the same site on two domains) and the LiveNet sign-in at auth.livenet.host. LiveNet Server Hosting and its panel at panel.livenet.host have their own privacy policy.

Who runs this

LiveNet is run by Livinglive234. Questions or requests about your data: livinglive234@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

When you create an account we store your username, email address, optional display name and a hashed password (never the password itself). If you add a passkey we store its public key, and if you link Google, Discord or GitHub we store the link between that account and yours. We do not receive or store your passwords for those services.

Beta requests

A request stores your name, your account email, what you plan to use LiveNet for, and your reason, plus the time you sent it. A copy is posted to a private LiveNet Discord channel so it can be reviewed.

Blog comments

Comments are shown publicly with your name and the time you posted. We also store your account ID so you can delete your own comments. A notification containing the comment is posted to a private Discord channel.

Cookies

We set one sign-in cookie so the site knows who you are. It is needed for the site to work and is not used for tracking. We run no advertising or analytics trackers.

Technical data

To stop abuse we keep short-lived rate-limit records tied to your account or network address. Our hosts may also keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and requested page).

How we use it

To run your account and sign you in, review beta requests, show comments, keep the site secure and stop spam and abuse, and contact you about your account or request. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and provides Turnstile, the bot check on sign-in, sign-up and forms. Turnstile receives information about your browser and connection to decide whether you are a person.
  • Discord receives beta requests and new-comment notifications in our private channels.
  • Google, Discord and GitHub are used only if you choose to sign in or link with them. Their own policies apply to your data there.
  • Have I Been Pwned is used to check new passwords against known breaches. Only a short, partial fingerprint of the password is sent, never the password or your email.
  • Email delivery: we send you emails such as confirmations and password resets through our mail provider.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists and is removed when it is deleted. Beta requests are kept until reviewed and may be deleted afterwards. Comments stay until you or an admin delete them, or the account is removed. Rate-limit records expire on their own.

Your choices

You can change your name, email, password, passkeys and linked accounts from your account page, and delete your own comments on the post they are on. To see, correct or delete your data, or to delete your account, email us and we will deal with it as soon as we reasonably can.

Children

LiveNet is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us information, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and we will tell account holders about significant changes.
